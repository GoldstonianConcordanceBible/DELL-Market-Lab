# DELL Bull Case

Reference Asset: DELL
Class ID: SYD-MKT-DELL-001

## Purpose

This document isolates the bullish DELL thesis so it can later be evaluated independently from the actual outcome.

## Bull Case Framework

The bullish thesis should be evaluated across five layers.

### 1. Fundamental

Revenue growth.

Earnings growth.

AI infrastructure demand.

Enterprise demand.

Server backlog.

Guidance.

Margins.

Free cash flow.

Capital allocation.

### 2. Sector

Semiconductor and AI infrastructure strength.

Data-center spending.

Enterprise technology spending.

Hyperscaler capital expenditure.

Hardware demand.

### 3. Technical

Breakout continuation.

Higher highs.

Higher lows.

VWAP support.

Reclaim of resistance.

Strong volume.

Relative strength.

### 4. Sentiment

Positive earnings reaction.

Analyst revisions.

Institutional interest.

Social attention.

Retail participation.

### 5. Risk / Reward

A bullish thesis is only actionable when the setup offers acceptable risk relative to expected reward.

A good company can still be a bad trade at the wrong price.

## Bull Confirmation

Bullish confirmation may include:

Reclaim and hold of resistance.

Strong opening range.

Sustained volume.

Successful retest.

Continuation above prior-session high.

Sector confirmation.

## Bull Invalidation

Potential invalidation may include:

Failed reclaim.

Loss of VWAP.

Breakdown below key support.

Weak volume.

Sector divergence.

Material negative catalyst.

## Core Principle

The bull case must survive evidence.

It does not survive simply because the researcher wants it to.
