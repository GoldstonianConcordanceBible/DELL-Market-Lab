## Educational Purpose

ENOCH ONE copytrade activity may be used as a live observational component of Stock Market Labs.

The purpose is to allow students to compare:

Control Tower thesis.

Actual executed positions.

Risk parameters.

Entry.

Exit.

Position management.

Benchmark performance.

Final outcome.

## Required Disclosure

The GDEX link used in this repository is a referral link.

The referring party may receive compensation associated with qualifying referral activity.

Copytrading is optional.

No student is required to copy a trade.

No student is required to deposit capital.

Paper observation remains a valid educational path.

## Risk

Copytrading does not eliminate trading risk.

Copied strategies may lose money.

Leveraged positions may amplify gains and losses.

Past performance does not guarantee future performance.

Students should independently choose their own risk parameters.
