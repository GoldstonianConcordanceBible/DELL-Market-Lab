## Purpose

The Control Tower preserves the decision process before, during, and after the market session.

The purpose is not to produce constant predictions.

The purpose is to maintain a disciplined record of:

What was known.

What was unknown.

What scenarios were possible.

What conditions would trigger action.

What conditions would invalidate action.

What actually occurred.

What should be learned afterward.

## Operating Principle

Do not predict one future when several futures remain possible.

Define branches.

Define evidence.

Define triggers.

Define invalidation.

Then allow the market to reveal which branch occurred.

## Control Tower Responsibilities

The Control Tower should distinguish:

FACT

Verified market information.

OBSERVATION

What can be directly seen in current market behavior.

ANALYSIS

Interpretation based on available evidence.

SCENARIO

A possible future path.

TRIGGER

The condition required before action is considered.

INVALIDATION

The condition that weakens or eliminates the setup.

OUTCOME

What later occurred.

## Relationship to Trading

The Control Tower does not require a trade.

NO TRADE is a valid outcome.

The best decision may be:

Wait.

Reduce risk.

Take partial profit.

Exit.

Re-enter later.

Observe only.

The objective is disciplined decision-making, not maximum activity.

## Relationship to $DLAB

The DELL Control Tower evaluates DELL independently.

DELL-equivalent value.

Liquidity.

## Core Principle

The market is allowed to disagree with the Control Tower.

When it does, preserve the disagreement.

That is evidence.
