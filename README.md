# Purpose
DELL Market Lab is an independent research and educational laboratory focused on the observation, documentation, and study of DELL as a reference asset.
The laboratory is organized around one governing question:
> **What did we know before the outcome?**
The objective is not to rewrite successful or unsuccessful market calls after the fact.
The objective is to preserve the research record.
DELL Market Lab distinguishes:
- what was known before an outcome,
- what changed during the event,
- what became known afterward,
- what originated as human interpretation,
- what came from AI-assisted analysis,
- what can be independently verified,
- and what remains uncertain.

# Evidence Classes
DELL Market Lab uses the following core evidence classifications.
## VERIFIED
Directly supported by an appropriate primary source, original record, or observable artifact.
Examples may include:
- official market data,
- company filings,
- platform receipts,
- timestamped recordings,
- verified transactions,
- original datasets,
- or authenticated records.
## CORROBORATED
Supported by multiple credible sources or by independent evidence consistent with an original observation.
## AUTHOR-ORIGINATED
An original interpretation, thesis, framework, commentary, or analytical conclusion.
A market thesis normally begins here.
## PROPOSED
Designed, recommended, or planned, but not yet operational or established.
## FUTURE EXPERIMENT
A planned experiment, intervention, comparison, or study that has not yet occurred.

## POST-EVENT
What became known after the relevant outcome?

A later outcome must not be used to silently rewrite an earlier thesis.

> Original pre-event artifact  
> ↓  
> Frozen  
> ↓

Model Agreement

MODEL AGREEMENT

* CORROBORATED,
* expert consensus,
* or proof of predictive skill.

Do not ask models after an outcome to reconstruct what they supposedly believed beforehand and insert those answers into a pre-event record.
