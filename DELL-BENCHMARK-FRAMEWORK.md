# DELL Benchmark Framework

Reference Asset: DELL
Companion Experiment: $DLAB
Class ID: SYD-MKT-DELL-001
Experiment ID: STOCKMEME-DELL-001

DELL is the reference asset.
$DLAB is the laboratory.

## Purpose

This document establishes the benchmark framework used to evaluate the DELL Market Lab.

The primary question is not simply:

Did $DLAB go up?

The more important question is:

Did participation in the laboratory outperform or underperform simply holding the DELL reference asset?

## Benchmark A — Cash / USD

This benchmark measures nominal value.

Record:

Initial USD-equivalent value.

Final USD-equivalent value.

Percentage return.

Purpose:

Determine whether the position gained or lost purchasing-power value in USD terms.

## Benchmark B — Hold DELL

This is the principal reference benchmark.

Assume an initial quantity of DELL is held without entering $DLAB.

Record:

DELL units at T0.

DELL price at T0.

DELL price at checkpoint.

USD-equivalent value at checkpoint.

This establishes the opportunity cost of entering the laboratory instead of simply holding the reference asset.

## Benchmark C — $DLAB Position

Record:

DELL committed to acquire $DLAB.

$DLAB units received.

Average acquisition price in DELL.

Current $DLAB price in DELL.

Estimated DELL received if converted back.

Fees.

Slippage.

Net DELL-equivalent value.

## Primary Relative-Performance Metric

DELL Accumulation Ratio

Formula:

DELL-equivalent value after exiting $DLAB
divided by
DELL originally committed

Interpretation:

Greater than 1.00:
The $DLAB position accumulated additional DELL units before considering any external tax consequences.

Equal to 1.00:
The $DLAB position approximately matched the number of DELL units originally committed.

Less than 1.00:
The $DLAB position lost DELL units relative to simply holding the reference asset.

## Example

Initial commitment:

10 DELL

Later $DLAB conversion value:

12 DELL

DELL Accumulation Ratio:

12 / 10 = 1.20

Interpretation:

The laboratory position accumulated approximately 20% more DELL units before fees and other costs.

If the same position later converts to:

8 DELL

DELL Accumulation Ratio:

8 / 10 = 0.80

Interpretation:

The laboratory position lost approximately 20% of its DELL-equivalent units relative to simply holding DELL.

## Dual Return Framework

Every $DLAB observation should preserve two returns.

### Relative Return

How did $DLAB perform against DELL?

Measured in DELL units.

### Nominal Return

How did $DLAB perform in USD-equivalent value?

Measured using the contemporaneous DELL reference value.

A $DLAB position can outperform DELL while still declining in USD terms.

A $DLAB position can underperform DELL while still gaining in USD terms.

Both measurements are required.

## Example Scenario A

DELL rises 20%.

$DLAB remains unchanged relative to DELL.

Result:

The $DLAB position may rise approximately with the DELL reference value.

Relative performance against DELL:

Approximately flat.

USD performance:

Positive.

## Example Scenario B

DELL rises 20%.

$DLAB rises 50% against DELL.

Result:

Both layers move favorably.

Relative performance:

Positive.

USD performance:

Potentially stronger than simply holding DELL.

## Example Scenario C

DELL rises 20%.

$DLAB falls 50% against DELL.

Result:

The reference asset rises while the laboratory loses substantial relative value.

The investor may have been better off simply holding DELL.

## Example Scenario D

DELL falls 30%.

$DLAB rises 20% against DELL.

Result:

$DLAB can outperform DELL on a relative basis while still losing USD-equivalent value.

This demonstrates why the quote asset and unit of account matter.

## Creator Benchmark

Creator fees should be measured separately from trader performance.

Record:

DELL creator fees received.

Time period.

Source transaction.

USD-equivalent value at receipt.

Subsequent change in DELL value.

Creator-fee revenue must not be treated as evidence that participants profited.

These are separate economic outcomes.
