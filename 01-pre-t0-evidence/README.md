Reference Asset: DELL

DELL is the reference asset.
$DLAB is the laboratory.

## Purpose

This directory assembles the evidence that existed before deployment of $DLAB.

The 00-provenance directory organizes evidence by source.

This directory organizes that evidence by chronology.

The purpose is to establish a frozen record of what existed before the companion token entered the experiment.

## T0 Definition

$DLAB T0 is the confirmed onchain deployment event.

T0 should be recorded using:

Transaction hash.

Block number.

Confirmed blockchain timestamp.

Contract address.

Creator wallet.

Pairing asset.

Final launch parameters.

Until that transaction exists, $DLAB T0 has not occurred.

## Pre-T0

Any artifact demonstrably created, published, observed, recorded, or committed before $DLAB T0 may qualify as Pre-T0 evidence.

Examples include:

DELL trading content.

DELL livestreams.

YouTube Shorts.

Control Tower ballots.

Trade records.

SydTek educational material.

Pons platform screenshots.

Social posts.

GitHub commits.

Market screenshots.

Research notes.

## Research Importance

The Pre-T0 record allows a future reviewer to distinguish:

What existed before the token.

What appeared during launch.

What happened after launch.

This protects the experiment from hindsight contamination.

## Core Claim

The DELL educational and trading case existed before $DLAB.

$DLAB is therefore being introduced as an additional experimental market layer around an existing DELL Market Lab record.

## Evidence Standard

Every Pre-T0 artifact should include enough information to determine:

What it is.

Where it came from.

When it existed.

Who produced it.

What it demonstrates.

What it does not demonstrate.

## Core Principle

The token does not create the history.

The history creates the experiment.
