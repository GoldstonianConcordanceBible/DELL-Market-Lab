## Purpose

DELL Market Lab is the Genesis implementation of the Stock Market Labs framework.

The project documents the complete chain from pre-outcome market analysis to live trading, education, social-market observation, independent onchain experimentation, and after-action review.

The purpose is not to predict outcomes after they happen.

The purpose is to preserve what was known before the outcome.

## Research Questions

1. What was known about DELL before the market outcome?

2. What trading setups were proposed before the outcome?

3. What actually happened in the DELL market?

4. How did $DLAB behave relative to DELL?

5. Did $DLAB accumulate or lose DELL units relative to simply holding DELL?

6. How did social attention change during the experiment?

7. How did educational engagement change?

8. Were correlations observed between the equity market, onchain market, social attention, and educational activity?

9. What conclusions are supported by evidence?

10. What remains unknown?

## Important Distinction
