DELL is the reference asset.
$DLAB is the laboratory.

## Purpose

This directory preserves the formal DELL market thesis used by the Genesis Market Lab.

The objective is to separate:

Fact.

Interpretation.

Scenario.

Trade setup.

Outcome.

The market thesis should explain why DELL is being studied, what conditions support the bull case, what conditions support the bear case, and what evidence would invalidate either view.

## Core Rule

A market thesis is not a promise.

A bullish thesis does not mean DELL must rise.

A bearish thesis does not mean DELL must fall.

The thesis defines a structured interpretation of the evidence available at the time.

## Required Elements

Every Market Lab thesis should include:

Reference asset.

Date.

Research horizon.

Known catalysts.

Bull case.

Bear case.

Neutral case.

Technical structure.

Fundamental context.

Macro context.

Sector context.

Risk factors.

Invalidation conditions.

Unknowns.

Benchmark.

Post-outcome review.

## Relationship to $DLAB

$DLAB is not itself the DELL thesis.

The DELL thesis concerns the reference asset.

The $DLAB experiment concerns an independent onchain market denominated in DELL.

These two systems may later be compared but should not be collapsed into one claim.

## Governing Principle

Study the stock first.

Study the laboratory second.
