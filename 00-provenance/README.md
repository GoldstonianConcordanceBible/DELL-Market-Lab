## Purpose

This directory preserves the evidence establishing what existed, what was observed, and what was published before and during the DELL Market Lab experiment.

Provenance is not an afterthought.

It is part of the experiment.

The goal is to maintain an auditable chronological record showing:

1. What existed before $DLAB.
2. What was known before market outcomes occurred.
3. What analysis was published before those outcomes.
4. What trades were livestreamed or otherwise documented.
5. What platform capabilities were directly observed.
6. What social and educational content existed before token deployment.
7. What changed after deployment.
8. What evidence supports each later conclusion.

## Core Rule

Evidence first.

Interpretation second.

Outcome third.

The historical record should never be rewritten simply because the market later revealed a different result.

Original evidence remains preserved.

Corrections receive new timestamps.

Later interpretations remain distinguishable from original observations.

## Evidence Classes

### VERIFIED

Evidence that can be independently checked through a public source, transaction, video, platform interface, timestamp, market record, or other reproducible source.

### CORROBORATED

Evidence supported by multiple independent records or sources.

### AUTHOR-ORIGINATED

Analysis, interpretation, commentary, thesis, or observation produced by Justin Goldston, ENOCH ONE, SydTek University, or another identified participant in the experiment.

### PROPOSED

A planned action, scenario, trading setup, research design, architecture, or hypothesis that had not yet occurred when recorded.

### FUTURE EXPERIMENT

A proposed measurement, outcome, comparison, or experiment that had not yet generated observed results.

## Research Integrity

A profitable trade does not erase a bad thesis.

A losing trade does not erase useful analysis.

A successful token does not prove causation.

A failed token does not invalidate the educational case.

A viral post does not prove it moved the market.

Correlation must not be presented as causation without evidence.

Each system must be measured independently.

DELL market performance.

$DLAB market performance.

Social attention.

Educational engagement.

Creator activity.

Onchain activity.
