## Purpose

This directory preserves screenshots that support the chronology, market observations, platform observations, trade record, social record, and educational record of DELL Market Lab.

Screenshots are supporting evidence.

Whenever a stronger primary source exists, preserve both.

Examples:

A Pons screenshot should be preserved alongside the related platform metadata.

A trade screenshot should be preserved alongside the trade record.

A YouTube screenshot should be preserved alongside the public URL.

A social screenshot should be preserved alongside the original post URL when available.

## Evidence Rule

Do not crop away information that materially affects interpretation.

Whenever possible preserve:

Timestamp

Platform

Asset

Account or wallet

Price

Quantity

Transaction hash

URL

Relevant context

## Privacy Rule

Do not publish private keys, seed phrases, passwords, authentication codes, personal addresses, or other credentials.

Wallet addresses and public transaction hashes may be preserved when they are intentionally part of the public research record.

## Chronology Rule

A screenshot captured after an event must not be represented as though it was captured before the event.

Record the actual capture date and, when known, the underlying event time.

## Integrity Rule

Do not modify screenshots in ways that change their evidentiary meaning.

Cropping for readability is acceptable only when the complete original is also preserved when practical.

Annotations should be stored as separate derivative files.

Example:

2026-09-03_DELL_ENTRY_01_ORIGINAL.jpg

2026-09-03_DELL_ENTRY_01_ANNOTATED.jpg

## Core Principle

A screenshot is a witness.

Its timestamp, source, and context determine what that witness can actually prove.
