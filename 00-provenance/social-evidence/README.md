## Research Principle

Attention is a measurable variable.

Attention is not automatically causation.

A viral post does not prove that DELL moved because of the post.

A viral post does not prove that $DLAB moved because of the post.

A rise in $DLAB does not prove that social engagement caused the move.

A rise in DELL does not prove that social attention caused the move.

The Market Lab records each system independently and compares them afterward.

## Core Rule

Do not treat follower count, views, token price, or trade P&L as interchangeable measures of success.

Each metric answers a different question.

## Provenance Doctrine

Preserve the original post.

Preserve the timestamp.

Preserve the URL.

Preserve the metrics at meaningful checkpoints.

Do not rewrite the caption after the outcome and represent it as the original record.

If a post is edited, preserve the original version when possible and document the edit.
