DELL is the reference asset.
$DLAB is the laboratory.

## Purpose

This directory preserves ENOCH ONE Control Tower analysis that existed before the relevant market outcome.

The purpose is to distinguish pre-outcome analysis from post-outcome explanation.

A Control Tower ballot should preserve:

Exact timestamp.

Reference asset.

Observed market condition.

Bull scenario.

Bear scenario.

No-trade scenario.

Entry trigger.

Invalidation.

Support.

Resistance.

Targets.

Known catalysts.

Unknowns.

Confidence or priority ranking where applicable.

Evidence sources.

Later outcome.

## Core Rule

The ballot must be frozen before the outcome is known.

A later interpretation must not overwrite the original ballot.

Corrections, revisions, and updated scenarios receive new timestamps.

## Evidence Principle

Prediction is evidence.

Invalidation is evidence.

Uncertainty is evidence.

A wrong forecast remains part of the record.

A correct forecast remains part of the record.

The objective is not to manufacture hindsight.

The objective is to preserve what was knowable at the time.

## Relationship to the Course

Students should be able to compare:

What ENOCH ONE proposed before the market opened.

What the trader executed.

What DELL actually did.

What $DLAB later did.

What social attention did.

What conclusions are justified afterward.

This creates a repeatable pre-outcome versus post-outcome learning framework.

## Canonical Doctrine

The evidence comes before the outcome.

The timestamp is the witness.
