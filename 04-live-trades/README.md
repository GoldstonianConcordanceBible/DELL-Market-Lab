## Purpose

This directory connects the pre-outcome Control Tower thesis to actual trade execution.

The objective is to preserve:

What setup was identified.

What triggered the trade.

What capital was committed.

What risk was accepted.

What happened while the position was open.

Why the position was reduced, held, or closed.

What the final result was.

## Core Rule

The trade record must remain separate from the later story told about the trade.

Execution should be judged against the plan that existed before entry.

## Trade Classification

REAL TRADE

Actual capital was committed.

PAPER TRADE

No actual capital was committed.

OBSERVATIONAL TRADE

The setup was documented but no position was opened.

## Minimum Record

Every live trade should preserve:

Ticker.

Direction.

Date.

Entry time.

Entry price.

Position size.

Capital committed.

Leverage if applicable.

Stop or invalidation.

Maximum planned risk.

Targets.

Exit time.

Exit price.

Realized P&L.

Return.

Evidence links.

Livestream link.

Control Tower ballot reference.

## Educational Principle

A profitable trade is not automatically a good trade.

A losing trade is not automatically a bad trade.

Process and outcome must be scored separately.

## Relationship to $DLAB

The DELL trade is one system.

$DLAB is another system.

A DELL trade should never be represented as a trade in $DLAB.

A $DLAB position should never be represented as ownership of DELL.

The two systems may later be compared through the benchmark framework.

## Core Principle

The setup creates the trade.

The receipt proves the trade.

The outcome teaches the lesson.
